## Accessible companion caption

Flashcard Studio turns Markdown notes into a study workflow inside Obsidian. The walkthrough shows a question, answer rating, an image-occlusion editor and a practice exam. Install from Community plugins on Obsidian 1.13.1 or later. Manual study is free and offline; optional AI generation may incur provider fees. Screenshots show sample data.
