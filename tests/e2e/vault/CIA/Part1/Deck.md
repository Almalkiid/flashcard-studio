#flashcards

**What is the purpose of internal auditing?**
?
- Provide **independent, objective assurance** and consulting
- Add value and improve operations
> [!tip] The board
> Internal audit reports functionally to the board.

**Who owns and manages risk in the Three Lines Model?**
?
- **Management**, in the first and second lines
- Internal audit is the **third line**
> [!tip] Independence
> Internal audit provides assurance on risk, it does not own it.

**What does CAE stand for?**
?
- **Chief Audit Executive**
- The head of the internal audit function
> [!tip] Reporting lines
> The CAE reports functionally to the board and administratively to senior management.

The CAE reports ==functionally== to the ==board==.
