# Code of conduct

Flashcard Studio is a small project that works best when people feel able to ask questions, report problems and suggest changes.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good faith. Criticise code, ideas and behaviour of the software, never the person.
- Keep discussion on topic and useful: say what you saw, what you expected and what would help.
- Respect other people's time. Maintainers work on this in their spare time, so allow a reasonable wait for an answer.
- Do not share other people's private information, including the contents of their notes or vaults, without their consent.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or discriminatory language.
- Personal attacks, trolling or deliberately derailing a discussion.
- Spam, advertising unrelated to the project, or posting malicious links.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may block repeat offenders from the repository. To report a problem, contact the maintainer, Almalkiid, through the contact details on [their GitHub profile](https://github.com/Almalkiid). Reports are handled privately.

This code of conduct applies to every space the project runs: issues, pull requests and discussions on GitHub.
